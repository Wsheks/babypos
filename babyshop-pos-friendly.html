A little help

Quick answers for the till. Nothing here can break a sale.

The M-PESA is slow, what do I do?
Tap Send again to nudge the phone. If the customer has already paid the till, tap Match a code and paste the M-PESA message. The sale only closes once the money shows.
The customer wants to pay half now, half later
On the Pay screen choose Split. Take what they have, the rest stays open and the till holds the items.
A button is missing for me
Some things, like giving a big discount or sending a refund, are set for managers only. Ask a manager to sign in, or the owner can turn it on for you in Settings.
The size the customer wants is out of stock
Tap the customer name and add a size reminder. They get a WhatsApp the day the next delivery lands.
